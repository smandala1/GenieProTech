Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, use, and protect your personal data when you use our services or visit our website. By accessing or using our service, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy.

1. Data Collection

We collect several types of personal data from users of our services, including:

  • Contact Information: This may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and other contact details.
  • Messaging Data: Any content shared with us via communication channels, such as emails, text messages, or other means.
  • Device and Usage Data: Automatically collected information such as your device’s IP address, browser type, operating system, and information on the pages you visit on our site, the duration of your visits, and other diagnostic data.

2. Data Usage

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • Service Improvement: To personalize your experience and improve our website and services.
  • Campaign Tracking & Analytics: To monitor the performance of our campaigns, assess user engagement, and optimize our offerings.
  • Communication: To send updates, respond to inquiries, and notify you about changes or promotions related to our services.
  • Marketing: To send marketing materials and promotional content about our services or third-party offerings that may be of interest to you.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties. All information will be used to provide and improve our services or for purposes you have agreed to.

3. Data Sharing

We may share your data in the following situations:

  • Service Providers: We may share your information with trusted third parties who help us operate our services, perform analytics, or manage communications.
  • Carriers for Registration: We may share necessary data with carriers or partners to facilitate registration processes or service access.
  • Business Transactions: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of our assets, your personal data may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different Privacy Policy.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your personal information to comply with legal obligations, enforce our policies, or protect our rights, property, or safety, and the rights, property, or safety of others.

4. Mobile Information Disclaimer

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. Opt-Out Policy

If at any point you no longer wish to receive communications from us, you can easily opt out by replying with STOP or QUIT to any message we send. This ensures that you have complete control over the communications you receive.

6. Data Protection

We take appropriate security measures to safeguard your personal data. However, please understand that no method of data transmission or storage can be guaranteed 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We will retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. Once no longer necessary, your personal data will be securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Consent Mechanism

Before collecting sensitive personal data, we will seek your consent through a simple opt-in process. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us or using the opt-out features provided.

9. External Partners Clarification

We may share your data with external partners or service providers who assist us with analytics, marketing, and other operational functions. These partners are bound by confidentiality agreements and will only use your data for the agreed-upon purposes.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take steps to remove that information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and we will notify you of significant updates through email or other prominent notices. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
